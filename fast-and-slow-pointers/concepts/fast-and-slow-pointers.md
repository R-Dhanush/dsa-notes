# Fast & Slow pointers
- Two pointers start at the same position and move forward through a "chained" structure (linked list, or a sequence where each step deterministically leads to the next).
- This is a specialization of the [same-direction](../../two-pointers/concepts/same-direction.md) two-pointers variant.
